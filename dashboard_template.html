Customer analytics · UK online retailer, Dec 2009 to Dec 2011

Who are the customers, what are they worth, and which ads work?

Two years of real transactions from a UK online gift retailer (UCI Online Retail II). Customers are segmented, their future spend is predicted and checked against what really happened, and seven ad-attribution methods are tested against a known answer.
